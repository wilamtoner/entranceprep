Essential Formula Reference Library

Quick reference tables for entrance exam equations and constants.

Physics Mechanics

  • Velocity: v = u + at
  • Displacement: s = ut + 1/2 at²
  • Work: W = F · s cos(θ)
  • Power: P = W / t = F · v

Physical Chemistry

  • Ideal Gas: PV = nRT
  • pH: pH = -log₁₀[H⁺]
  • Molarity: M = moles of solute / Liters of solution
  • Boyle's Law: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂

Mathematics & Calculus

  • Quadratic Formula: x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / (2a)
  • Derivative d/dx[xⁿ]: n · xⁿ⁻¹
  • Trig Identity: sin²(x) + cos²(x) = 1
  • Log Product Rule: log(ab) = log(a) + log(b)
