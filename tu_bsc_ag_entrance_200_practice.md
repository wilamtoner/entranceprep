## Physics

1. The SI unit of force is:
A. Joule  B. Newton  C. Watt  D. Pascal
Answer: B
Guide: Force = mass x acceleration.

2. A body moving with uniform velocity has:
A. Zero acceleration  B. Constant acceleration  C. Increasing acceleration  D. Variable acceleration
Answer: A
Guide: Uniform velocity means speed and direction are constant.

3. The slope of a velocity-time graph gives:
A. Displacement  B. Acceleration  C. Momentum  D. Force
Answer: B
Guide: Acceleration is rate of change of velocity.

4. Momentum is equal to:
A. mv  B. ma  C. mg  D. m/v
Answer: A
Guide: Linear momentum = mass x velocity.

5. Work done is zero when force and displacement are:
A. Parallel  B. Opposite  C. Perpendicular  D. Equal
Answer: C
Guide: W = Fs cos theta.

6. The energy possessed by a body due to motion is:
A. Potential energy  B. Kinetic energy  C. Heat energy  D. Chemical energy
Answer: B
Guide: KE = 1/2 mv^2.

7. Power is the rate of:
A. Force  B. Momentum  C. Work done  D. Pressure
Answer: C
Guide: Power = work/time.

8. The acceleration due to gravity on Earth is approximately:
A. 1.6 m/s^2  B. 3.7 m/s^2  C. 9.8 m/s^2  D. 10.8 m/s^2
Answer: C
Guide: Use g = 9.8 m/s^2 unless approximated as 10.

9. Pressure in a liquid increases with:
A. Decrease in depth  B. Increase in depth  C. Decrease in density  D. Decrease in gravity
Answer: B
Guide: P = h rho g.

10. Archimedes' principle is related to:
A. Heat flow  B. Buoyancy  C. Electricity  D. Magnetism
Answer: B
Guide: Upthrust equals weight of displaced fluid.

11. Heat transfer in solids mainly occurs by:
A. Conduction  B. Convection  C. Radiation  D. Evaporation
Answer: A
Guide: Conduction is dominant in solids.

12. The specific heat capacity of water is high, so water:
A. Heats and cools quickly  B. Heats and cools slowly  C. Cannot absorb heat  D. Has no temperature
Answer: B
Guide: High specific heat moderates temperature change.

13. The unit of electric current is:
A. Volt  B. Ohm  C. Ampere  D. Coulomb
Answer: C
Guide: Current is measured in amperes.

14. Ohm's law is:
A. V = IR  B. P = VI  C. Q = It  D. F = ma
Answer: A
Guide: Voltage equals current times resistance.

15. In a series circuit, current is:
A. Different through each resistor  B. Same through all resistors  C. Zero  D. Infinite
Answer: B
Guide: Series path has one current path.

16. A fuse wire should have:
A. High melting point  B. Low melting point  C. Infinite resistance  D. Zero resistance
Answer: B
Guide: It melts during excess current.

17. The magnetic field around a straight current-carrying conductor is:
A. Straight lines  B. Circular lines  C. Random lines  D. No field
Answer: B
Guide: Use right-hand thumb rule.

18. The frequency of sound audible to humans is about:
A. 2-20 Hz  B. 20-20000 Hz  C. 20000-50000 Hz  D. Above 50000 Hz only
Answer: B
Guide: Human audible range is 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

19. Sound cannot travel through:
A. Air  B. Water  C. Steel  D. Vacuum
Answer: D
Guide: Sound needs a material medium.

20. The image formed by a plane mirror is:
A. Real and inverted  B. Virtual and erect  C. Real and erect  D. Virtual and inverted
Answer: B
Guide: Plane mirror image is virtual, erect, same size.

21. A convex lens is also called:
A. Diverging lens  B. Converging lens  C. Plane lens  D. Reflecting lens
Answer: B
Guide: Convex lens converges parallel rays.

22. The defect corrected by a concave lens is:
A. Hypermetropia  B. Myopia  C. Astigmatism only  D. Cataract
Answer: B
Guide: Myopia = short-sightedness.

23. The refractive index of a medium is:
A. c/v  B. v/c  C. cv  D. c+v
Answer: A
Guide: Refractive index compares speed in vacuum to medium.

24. The half-life of a radioactive substance is the time required for:
A. Complete decay  B. Half of nuclei to decay  C. Double nuclei to form  D. Temperature to halve
Answer: B
Guide: Half-life is a nuclear decay concept.

25. Which particle has negative charge?
A. Proton  B. Neutron  C. Electron  D. Alpha particle
Answer: C
Guide: Electrons are negatively charged.

26. The principle of a transformer is:
A. Mutual induction  B. Reflection  C. Refraction  D. Diffusion
Answer: A
Guide: Transformers work with changing magnetic flux.

27. A step-up transformer increases:
A. Current only  B. Voltage  C. Resistance only  D. Frequency
Answer: B
Guide: Step-up means secondary voltage is greater.

28. The device used to measure atmospheric pressure is:
A. Ammeter  B. Barometer  C. Hydrometer  D. Voltmeter
Answer: B
Guide: Barometer measures air pressure.

29. Surface tension is due to:
A. Nuclear force  B. Cohesive force between molecules  C. Gravity only  D. Electric current
Answer: B
Guide: Molecules at a liquid surface experience inward pull.

30. The dimensional formula of velocity is:
A. LT^-1  B. LT^-2  C. ML^-1T^-2  D. MLT^-1
Answer: A
Guide: Velocity = distance/time.

## Chemistry

31. Atomic number equals the number of:
A. Neutrons  B. Protons  C. Protons plus neutrons  D. Electrons plus neutrons
Answer: B
Guide: Atomic number identifies the element.

32. Isotopes have the same:
A. Mass number  B. Atomic number  C. Number of neutrons  D. Physical mass
Answer: B
Guide: Isotopes differ in neutron number.

33. The maximum number of electrons in the second shell is:
A. 2  B. 4  C. 8  D. 18
Answer: C
Guide: Use 2n^2 rule.

34. Ionic bonds are formed by:
A. Sharing electrons  B. Transfer of electrons  C. Sharing protons  D. Transfer of neutrons
Answer: B
Guide: Ionic compounds form ions.

35. Covalent compounds generally have:
A. Low melting points  B. Very high conductivity  C. Metallic lustre  D. Free ions in solid state
Answer: A
Guide: Many simple covalent substances have weak intermolecular forces.

36. pH less than 7 indicates:
A. Acidic solution  B. Basic solution  C. Neutral solution  D. Salt only
Answer: A
Guide: Acids have pH below 7.

37. A strong acid in water:
A. Does not ionize  B. Partially ionizes  C. Completely ionizes  D. Forms only OH- ions
Answer: C
Guide: Strong acids dissociate almost completely.

38. The formula of washing soda is:
A. NaHCO3  B. Na2CO3.10H2O  C. CaCO3  D. NaCl
Answer: B
Guide: Washing soda is hydrated sodium carbonate.

39. The formula of baking soda is:
A. NaHCO3  B. Na2CO3  C. CaO  D. KCl
Answer: A
Guide: Sodium bicarbonate is baking soda.

40. Oxidation is:
A. Gain of electrons  B. Loss of electrons  C. Gain of protons  D. Loss of neutrons
Answer: B
Guide: OIL RIG: oxidation is loss.

41. Reduction is:
A. Gain of electrons  B. Loss of electrons  C. Loss of hydrogen  D. Gain of oxygen only
Answer: A
Guide: Reduction involves electron gain.

42. Avogadro's number is approximately:
A. 6.022 x 10^20  B. 6.022 x 10^23  C. 3.14 x 10^23  D. 9.8 x 10^23
Answer: B
Guide: One mole contains 6.022 x 10^23 particles.

43. One mole of oxygen gas at STP occupies:
A. 11.2 L  B. 22.4 L  C. 44.8 L  D. 1 L
Answer: B
Guide: Molar gas volume at STP is 22.4 L.

44. The gas liberated when acids react with metals is:
A. Oxygen  B. Hydrogen  C. Nitrogen  D. Carbon dioxide
Answer: B
Guide: Metal + acid gives salt + hydrogen.

45. The gas evolved when carbonates react with acids is:
A. CO2  B. H2  C. O2  D. NH3
Answer: A
Guide: Carbonates release carbon dioxide.

46. The main component of natural gas is:
A. Methane  B. Ethane  C. Propane  D. Butane
Answer: A
Guide: Natural gas is mostly methane.

47. The functional group of alcohols is:
A. -COOH  B. -OH  C. -CHO  D. -CO-
Answer: B
Guide: Alcohols contain hydroxyl group.

48. The general formula of alkanes is:
A. CnH2n  B. CnH2n+2  C. CnH2n-2  D. CnHn
Answer: B
Guide: Alkanes are saturated hydrocarbons.

49. Ethene gives a positive test with:
A. Bromine water  B. Lime water only  C. Blue litmus  D. Starch solution
Answer: A
Guide: Unsaturated compounds decolorize bromine water.

50. The catalyst used in Haber process is:
A. Nickel  B. Iron  C. Vanadium pentoxide  D. Platinum
Answer: B
Guide: Haber process makes ammonia.

51. The catalyst used in contact process is:
A. Iron  B. Nickel  C. V2O5  D. MnO2
Answer: C
Guide: Contact process makes sulfuric acid.

52. Hard water contains salts of:
A. Sodium and potassium  B. Calcium and magnesium  C. Silver and gold  D. Hydrogen and oxygen
Answer: B
Guide: Ca2+ and Mg2+ cause hardness.

53. Temporary hardness is removed by:
A. Boiling  B. Freezing  C. Distillation only  D. Adding sugar
Answer: A
Guide: Bicarbonates decompose on boiling.

54. The ore of aluminium is:
A. Hematite  B. Bauxite  C. Galena  D. Cinnabar
Answer: B
Guide: Aluminium is extracted from bauxite.

55. Rusting of iron requires:
A. Oxygen and water  B. Nitrogen only  C. Carbon dioxide only  D. Dry air only
Answer: A
Guide: Rusting is oxidation in moist air.

56. An alloy of copper and zinc is:
A. Bronze  B. Brass  C. Steel  D. Solder
Answer: B
Guide: Brass = Cu + Zn.

57. The noble gas used in electric bulbs is:
A. Argon  B. Chlorine  C. Oxygen  D. Hydrogen
Answer: A
Guide: Inert gas prevents filament oxidation.

58. The most electronegative element is:
A. Chlorine  B. Oxygen  C. Fluorine  D. Nitrogen
Answer: C
Guide: Fluorine has highest electronegativity.

59. A buffer solution resists change in:
A. Mass  B. pH  C. Volume  D. Colour only
Answer: B
Guide: Buffers maintain pH.

60. The chemical formula of urea is:
A. NH4NO3  B. CO(NH2)2  C. KNO3  D. CaCO3
Answer: B
Guide: Urea is a nitrogen fertilizer.

## Botany

61. The basic unit of life is:
A. Tissue  B. Cell  C. Organ  D. Organ system
Answer: B
Guide: All living organisms are made of cells.

62. Cell wall in plants is mainly made of:
A. Chitin  B. Cellulose  C. Protein  D. Glycogen
Answer: B
Guide: Plant cell wall provides support.

63. The site of photosynthesis is:
A. Mitochondria  B. Chloroplast  C. Ribosome  D. Nucleus
Answer: B
Guide: Chlorophyll is in chloroplasts.

64. The main photosynthetic pigment is:
A. Carotene  B. Xanthophyll  C. Chlorophyll a  D. Anthocyanin
Answer: C
Guide: Chlorophyll a is primary pigment.

65. The gas released during photosynthesis is:
A. CO2  B. O2  C. N2  D. H2
Answer: B
Guide: Oxygen comes from photolysis of water.

66. Stomata are mainly responsible for:
A. Mineral absorption  B. Gas exchange  C. Seed formation  D. Protein synthesis
Answer: B
Guide: Stomata also regulate transpiration.

67. Xylem transports:
A. Food  B. Water and minerals  C. Hormones only  D. Pollen
Answer: B
Guide: Xylem movement is mostly upward.

68. Phloem transports:
A. Water only  B. Food  C. Oxygen only  D. Soil particles
Answer: B
Guide: Phloem carries sugars from source to sink.

69. The region of active cell division in root is:
A. Root cap  B. Meristematic zone  C. Maturation zone  D. Cork
Answer: B
Guide: Meristems divide actively.

70. Root hairs help in:
A. Photosynthesis  B. Absorption of water  C. Flowering  D. Seed dispersal
Answer: B
Guide: Root hairs increase surface area.

71. Nitrogen fixation is performed by:
A. Rhizobium  B. Yeast  C. Penicillium  D. Amoeba
Answer: A
Guide: Rhizobium lives in legume root nodules.

72. The edible part of potato is:
A. Root  B. Stem tuber  C. Leaf  D. Fruit
Answer: B
Guide: Potato is a modified underground stem.

73. Ginger is a:
A. Rhizome  B. Bulb  C. Corm  D. Tendril
Answer: A
Guide: Rhizome is a horizontal underground stem.

74. Onion is a:
A. Tuber  B. Bulb  C. Rhizome  D. Runner
Answer: B
Guide: Bulbs have fleshy scale leaves.

75. Tendrils in pea are modified:
A. Roots  B. Leaves  C. Flowers  D. Fruits
Answer: B
Guide: Pea tendrils are leaf modifications.

76. A flower with both androecium and gynoecium is:
A. Unisexual  B. Bisexual  C. Sterile  D. Incomplete only
Answer: B
Guide: Bisexual flowers have both sex organs.

77. Transfer of pollen from anther to stigma is:
A. Fertilization  B. Pollination  C. Germination  D. Transpiration
Answer: B
Guide: Pollination precedes fertilization.

78. Double fertilization is characteristic of:
A. Algae  B. Bryophytes  C. Gymnosperms  D. Angiosperms
Answer: D
Guide: Angiosperms form zygote and endosperm.

79. Fruit develops from:
A. Ovary  B. Anther  C. Stigma  D. Sepal
Answer: A
Guide: Ovule becomes seed.

80. Seed dormancy means:
A. Rapid growth  B. Temporary failure to germinate  C. Death of embryo  D. Flowering
Answer: B
Guide: Dormancy helps survival under unfavorable conditions.

81. Monocot seeds usually have:
A. One cotyledon  B. Two cotyledons  C. No embryo  D. No endosperm
Answer: A
Guide: Maize and rice are monocots.

82. Dicot leaves commonly show:
A. Parallel venation  B. Reticulate venation  C. No venation  D. Circular venation
Answer: B
Guide: Dicots usually have net-like veins.

83. Bryophytes are called amphibians of plant kingdom because:
A. They live only in sea  B. They need water for reproduction  C. They have flowers  D. They produce seeds
Answer: B
Guide: Motile sperm require water.

84. Vascular tissue first appeared in:
A. Algae  B. Bryophytes  C. Pteridophytes  D. Fungi
Answer: C
Guide: Pteridophytes are seedless vascular plants.

85. Gymnosperms have:
A. Naked seeds  B. Fruits  C. Flowers  D. No vascular tissue
Answer: A
Guide: Seeds are not enclosed in fruit.

86. Fungi store food as:
A. Starch  B. Glycogen  C. Cellulose  D. Sucrose
Answer: B
Guide: Fungi resemble animals in reserve food.

87. Yeast reproduces mainly by:
A. Budding  B. Fragmentation  C. Spore formation only  D. Binary fission only
Answer: A
Guide: Budding is common in yeast.

88. Virus contains:
A. Only cell wall  B. Nucleic acid and protein coat  C. Chloroplast  D. Mitochondria
Answer: B
Guide: Viruses are acellular.

89. Mendel worked on:
A. Garden pea  B. Maize  C. Wheat  D. Rice
Answer: A
Guide: Pisum sativum was Mendel's experimental plant.

90. The phenotypic ratio in monohybrid F2 cross is:
A. 1:1  B. 3:1  C. 9:3:3:1  D. 1:2:1
Answer: B
Guide: Dominant to recessive phenotype is 3:1.

91. The genotypic ratio in monohybrid F2 cross is:
A. 3:1  B. 1:2:1  C. 9:3:3:1  D. 2:1
Answer: B
Guide: TT:Tt:tt = 1:2:1.

92. Auxin helps in:
A. Stem elongation  B. Fruit ripening only  C. Leaf fall only  D. Seed dormancy only
Answer: A
Guide: Auxin promotes cell elongation.

93. Ethylene is mainly associated with:
A. Root absorption  B. Fruit ripening  C. Photosynthesis  D. Xylem transport
Answer: B
Guide: Ethylene is a gaseous plant hormone.

94. Gibberellin promotes:
A. Stem elongation and seed germination  B. Stomatal closure only  C. Leaf death only  D. Root hair death
Answer: A
Guide: GA breaks dormancy in many seeds.

95. Transpiration mainly occurs through:
A. Root hairs  B. Stomata  C. Xylem vessels only  D. Seeds
Answer: B
Guide: Stomatal transpiration is major.
